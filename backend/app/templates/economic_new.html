Want uncertainty ranges instead of one exact number?

Optional — leave this out and you get exactly what's above. Add a top-level "uncertainty" block and the result includes a P10/P50/P90 spread plus a note on what it's most sensitive to:

"uncertainty": {
  "probability": {"low": 0.15, "high": 0.30},
  "cost_multiplier": {"low": 0.85, "high": 1.15}
}

probability ranges how likely disruption is; cost_multiplier ranges how far off your total cost estimate could be. Neither is guessed for you — both come only from the numbers you put in this block.
