What this map will not tell you

A marker shows the current global risk level and whether that route is already inside a disruption. It is not a forecast for that route — the signal tells you escalation risk is rising somewhere, not where. There are no vessel positions here either: we make no claim about traffic volumes, so showing ships would imply one.
